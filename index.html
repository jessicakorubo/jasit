Our product

SmartLMS: the school management system we built ourselves

SmartLMS runs the daily operations of Nigerian private schools — students, fees, results, attendance and CBT exams — all from one platform.

  • Student records, fees and results in one system
  • Built-in CBT exams for WAEC & NECO prep
  • Live in a few days, with hands-on onboarding
Explore SmartLMS

> results: auto-compiled ✓
