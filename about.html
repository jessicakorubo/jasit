about/jasit

A tech company that teaches, builds, and ships

JASIT Technologies is based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. We train developers, build our own software products, and build for clients — all under one roof.

Our story

Built as a software team, grew into a school

JASIT started by building software — including SmartLMS, our school management system now used by Nigerian private schools. Along the way, we kept meeting people who wanted to learn how to build things themselves.

That's how our courses came about. Today, JASIT operates as three connected parts of one company: a place to learn, a product we run, and a team that builds for clients.
