Everything a school needs

One platform. Every school operation.

No more scattered spreadsheets, paper registers, or lost fee receipts. SmartLMS centralises everything your school runs on.

Student information management

Register, track, and manage all your students with profiles, class assignments, and complete academic history.

School fee management

Set fee categories, track partial payments per student, generate receipts, and know exactly who owes what.

CBT examination engine

Create and administer computer-based tests with automatic marking, instant results and analytics. Preps students for WAEC and NECO CBT.

Result compilation & report cards

Generate result sheets and report cards automatically at end of term — no manual calculations, no errors.

Attendance tracking

Mark and monitor daily student and teacher attendance for every class — no paper registers needed.

Teacher & student portals

Teachers upload schemes of work and grades; students access results, assignments and CBT exams.
